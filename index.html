About the school

Tiong San, since 1925

Sun Yat Sen High School of Iloilo was founded in 1925 by members of Iloilo's Filipino-Chinese community and named in honor of Dr. Sun Yat-sen. Alumni and families still call it by its Hokkien name, Tiong San.

Run by a Board of Trustees, the school has grown on Mapa Street into two four-storey buildings, joined by a bridge over the street that the community built through donations in 2009 to 2010. After more than a century, it continues to serve Ilonggo families across generations.

    Admissions

    Join the Blue Lions

    We welcome new students from Preschool through Senior High. Visit the Registrar's Office on Mapa Street to begin.

    1. Inquire. Visit or contact the Registrar's Office.
    2. Submit requirements. Report card (Form 138), PSA birth certificate, certificate of good moral character, and 2x2 photos.
    3. Assessment. Take the entrance assessment if your grade level requires it.
    4. Enroll. Pay the enrollment fee and receive your class schedule.
